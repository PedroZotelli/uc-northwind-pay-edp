Explicit accept, reviewer, reason

Topology crosses to READY only through a human

The only route to READY is review --accept --reviewer … --reason … — an explicit accept, a named reviewer, a stated reason.

Human topology barrierNo machine step can produce READY. A person reads the exact topology bytes and accepts them — or the plan stays at NEEDS_REVIEW.
seamwise --workspace "/path/to/project" review \ --accept --reviewer "human-name" \ --reason "Topology accepted."
Refusals — fail closed
  • No implicit review — absence of a command is not consent
  • No blank reviewer — the accept must name a human
  • No blank reason — the accept must say why
  • No OPEN objection — objections resolve first
  • No changed seam map — drift invalidates the draft
