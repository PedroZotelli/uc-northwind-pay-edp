CLAIM POLICY May say · must refuse CONVERGE · 0.2.0

Twelve claims this book may make. Eleven it must refuse.

Claim discipline is part of the product — every page that follows is bound by this policy (condensed from §2; the brief rows stay authoritative).

may make · green claims 12 claims
  • A thin factory coordinator around independently versioned Seamwise and Task-Spec binaries.
  • Each decision has one authority; duplicate capability is tolerated, duplicate authority is not.
  • Nine passes, two phases, one barrier; Capture optional, Register opt-in.
  • Materialization never authorizes dispatch — the receipt records dispatch_authorized: false.
  • taskspec gate --stamp is the only engine action that seals a leaf for delegation.
  • Bind adds a repository fence that may be stricter than the Task-Spec write scope.
  • The loop chooses no work — one named issue under finite iteration, time, token, path budgets.
  • Default isolation is a Git worktree; settled work survives on a task branch.
  • Eight distinct terminal outcomes: LOCAL_SETTLED, SETTLED, NO_OP, BLOCKED, STALLED, EXHAUSTED, CANCELLED, ERROR.
  • Task-Spec performs independent acceptance of the exact handoff and revision; Converge does not reimplement it.
  • Cockpit and Ask cannot authorize or settle — observation and interpretation only.
  • Immutable v0.2.0 release and current main are different evidence corridors.
must refuse · red claims 11 refusals
  • “Everyday portfolio coordinator” — OPERATING.md assigns that plane to WorkHelm.
  • Converge owns Seamwise or Task-Spec internals (decomposition, authorization, acceptance).
  • A reviewed topology, materialized task, green narration, or composition receipt authorizes execution.
  • HMAC proves identity, hides the key, or sandboxes — it is tamper evidence under a shared key.
  • Bind prevents every out-of-scope write — some adapters only detect after the fact.
  • Tier-2 holdouts are secret from the worker — the brief still points the worker at the Task-Spec.
  • LOCAL_SETTLED means a PR opened, remote state changed, production healthy, or a merge.
  • Cockpit, Ask, cvg next, or a tracker projection is a second source of canonical state.
  • Manager fleet, autonomous fan-out, live-tracker or production reliability ships in v0.2.0.
  • Checkout-only guided chat and the 60-form CLI are committed, released, or hosted.
  • Current make check passes — the layout gate is red.
