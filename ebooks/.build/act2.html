THE DECISION · OWNER + RATIONALE + STATUS

A cited decision must be accepted before its seams are ready

A seam may cite decision IDs — but every cited decision must be accepted and carry a nonblank owner and rationale before any dependent seam turns ready.

Cited decision · proposed or blank
Dependent seams stay blocked
A decision that is only proposed — or is missing its owner or rationale — keeps every seam that cites it short of READY.
vs
Cited decision · accepted
Dependent seams unlock
Accepted status with a nonblank owner and rationale releases the seams that cite the decision.

THE OBJECTION · MODELS MAY RAISE; OWNERS RESOLVE

Models may raise objections; only owners resolve them

OPEN blocks review. FIXED or ACCEPTED both require an owner and a rationale — there is no path out of an objection without a named human ruling.

stateDiagram-v2 [*] --> OPEN OPEN --> FIXED: owner + repair + rationale OPEN --> ACCEPTED: owner + explicit risk acceptance FIXED --> [*] ACCEPTED --> [*]
OPEN
Blocks review
Raised by model or human; the default state until an owner rules.
FIXED
Owner + repair + rationale
The objection was repaired; the owner records what changed and why.
ACCEPTED
Owner + explicit risk acceptance
The objection stands as accepted risk — owned, reasoned, reviewable.
