Decompose · the grammar of the cut

A seam is a crossing,
not a work assignment.

A true seam changes behavior without editing everything around it. A false seam cuts meaning — and no tool owns meaning. SeamWise maps the real joints, then gives each one a lane.

SEAM
the crossing
Where two systems hand off — you can change what happens on one side without editing the other. Evidence decides where it falls.
SWIMLANE
the owner
One lane inside the seam, one owner. Ownership is singular or it is nothing — a lane with two owners is a seam that was not cut.
LEG
the capability state
One observable state in the lane — staged, reconciled, served. States, never activities: each leg can be checked, not just done.
Topology · dependency · contention · lineage

The plan is a shape,
not a list.

Legs are not ordered by hope. SeamWise proves dependency ordering, serializes contention, and binds every unit to its seam, its lane, its leg and its source digest.

Compile fails closed.

No current review receipt, no compile. The receipt binds to the exact plan digest — change the plan and the receipt goes stale. A rerun produces identical bytes, or nothing at all.

Lineage travels with the work: every unit id names its seam, lane and leg — boundary, owner, order and proof stay attached, or the split was confetti.
